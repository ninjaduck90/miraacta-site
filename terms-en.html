2. What the app does

Miraacta works directly with the files in the working folder you choose: it reads files, renames and moves them, moves deleted files to the Trash, and stores additional information (tags, invoice data, recognised text) in the .mira subfolder. Miraacta does not run its own server and keeps no copies of your documents.

The app is free to download and can be tried with all features for 14 days. After that, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks the full version. Without the full version you can still view, search, open and share your documents.

Syncing through iCloud Drive or another cloud service is not a backup: changes and deletions are carried over to all devices. Back up your documents in particular before renaming or moving many files at once.
