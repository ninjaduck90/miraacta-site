From letter to findable file in three steps

You do not have to invent a filing system. Miraacta suggests a name when you save, and you decide.

  1. Scan or import

    Photograph a document with iPhone or iPad, including several pages. Bring in existing PDFs and images on any device by importing them.

  2. Name and tag

    Type, date and name form a consistent file name. The app recognizes date, amount and bank details in the text and fills them in.

  3. Find it again

    Search for a word that appears anywhere in the document, or filter by tags. The recognized text of every page is searchable.

What Miraacta does for your paperwork

Made for household paperwork: invoices, contracts, official notices, medical letters.

Full-text search

Text recognition runs on your device. After that you find an invoice by its customer number or a notice by its reference number.

Invoices at a glance

Mark invoices as open or paid and add the due date. A filter shows what is still open or overdue.

Your own naming scheme

You define the document types and whether the name starts with type, date or title. Existing files can be adjusted to the scheme.

Inbox with rules

Drop files into an inbox folder. Rules assign tags based on words that appear in the text.

Find duplicates

The app detects files with identical content, even under different names, and lists them for cleanup.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

If the folder is in iCloud Drive, you see the same state on all devices, including tags and invoice status.

Your documents stay in your folder

Miraacta is not a cloud service. You choose a folder, and the app works with the files in it. Everything it notes about them sits next to them in the .mira subfolder.

  • No user account, no sign-in.
  • No server of ours: your documents are not sent to us.
  • Ordinary PDF files that you can open without the app.

Help and contact

Support

Questions about the app, a bug or privacy: support@blockfamilie.de

Restore your purchase

New device or reinstalled: in the app under “More”, then “Full version…” and “Restore purchases”. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups

Miraacta works directly with the files in your workspace folder: it renames them, moves them and puts deleted files in the trash. Please back up your documents regularly, for example with Time Machine or a copy on an external drive.

Syncing through iCloud Drive is not a backup: if a file is deleted on one device, it disappears on all of them.
