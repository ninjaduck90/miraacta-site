Privacy Policy for Miraacta

Effective: 28 September 2026

Controller: Jan Marc Block. This policy applies to Miraacta on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and to this website.

No proprietary cloud, no tracking

Miraacta does not run its own servers for your documents and does not require an account. There is no tracking and no advertising. The app does not send your documents to third parties for analysis. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Where your data lives

Documents stay in the working folder you choose (for example iCloud Drive or a local folder). Extra metadata such as tags, invoice status, notes, scan settings, and OCR full text is stored in a hidden .mira folder inside that same working folder. This folder travels with your files, including via iCloud if you choose an iCloud folder.

A device-local search index (SQLite) lives in the app sandbox and can be rebuilt at any time. It is not a substitute for the working folder.

Permissions

  • Files and folders: only folders you explicitly select.
  • Camera: only when you use Scan to save a document as PDF.
  • iCloud Drive: only if you choose an iCloud folder as the working folder. Sync is provided by Apple, not by Miraacta.

Deletion

Uninstalling Miraacta removes the local search index. Your documents and the .mira folder remain in the working folder until you delete them there. Removing a working folder inside the app does not delete the files.

Website

When you open these pages, the web server may process ordinary connection data (IP address, time, requested address) as needed to deliver the page. No profiles are built.
